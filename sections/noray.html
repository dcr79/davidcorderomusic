Noray Records is an independent record label founded in 2025 and dedicated to ambient music. Since its inception, its goal has been to release music with a strong artistic identity, always prioritizing creative honesty.

Noray works with artists from different countries, bringing together a catalogue defined by calmness, attention to detail and a thoughtful approach to sound. Design and presentation are also an essential part of the label's identity. Alongside digital releases, selected releases are also available on cassette.
