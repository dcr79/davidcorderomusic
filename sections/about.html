David Cordero is a Spanish composer and sound artist whose work explores stillness, memory and the emotional resonance of place. His music blends acoustic instruments, field recordings and subtle electronic textures into intimate, cinematic soundscapes.

For more than two decades he has released a large body of work on labels around the world, developing a distinctive voice within contemporary ambient music. His compositions have accompanied films, exhibitions and multidisciplinary artistic projects, always with a focus on atmosphere, detail and emotional depth.

Alongside his solo work, he is the founder of Noray Records, an independent label dedicated to contemporary ambient and experimental music, supporting artists whose work shares a quiet and deeply personal approach to sound.
