David Cordero is a composer, producer and sound artist whose work focuses on contemporary ambient music. For over two decades, he has developed a distinctive musical language in which electronics, acoustic instruments and field recordings coexist naturally in the service of composition.

Throughout his career, he has released an extensive body of work on some of the most respected labels in the international ambient scene. He has also collaborated with artists from different countries and composed music for films, documentaries and art installations.

Alongside his work as a composer, he runs Noray Records, an independent label dedicated to releasing both his own music and the work of artists who share a similar artistic vision. Its catalogue brings together projects from different countries, united by a common artistic sensibility.
